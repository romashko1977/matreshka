# Серия 3. «Посылка»

**Сюжет:** Чебу Романович получает посылку. Внутри говорящий попугай, который с первого
взгляда в него влюбляется. Чебу пытается от него избавиться, но в итоге оставляет.

**Длительность:** ~65 секунд, 8 сцен.
**Референсы:** Чебу Романович, Валькирия, Дирк, попугай (`characters/parrot.md`).
Если герой «поплыл» внешне, добавить в начало промта полный блок из его карточки.

---

## Сцена 1. Ящик на крыльце (8 сек)
**Референс:** Чебу

```
Morning, the porch of a cozy house with climbing roses. The front door opens and a world-weary
furry creature with realistic brown plush fur, huge round ears, a scar over the left eyebrow,
flat cap, pinstripe vest and a cigar in his mouth, steps out and freezes. On the doorstep sits
a wooden crate with air holes, stamped in red Cyrillic letters "ОСТОРОЖНО. ЖИВОЕ". He looks left
and right suspiciously, narrowing his eyes. Medium shot, warm morning light, cinematic 3D,
comedic tension.
Dialogue in Russian, with lip-sync:
CHEBU (low, husky, calm male voice, slow): "Я ничего не заказывал."
```

## Сцена 2. Вскрытие (8 сек)
**Референс:** Чебу

```
Kitchen table, warm light. The furry creature in the flat cap carefully pries open the wooden
crate with a small crowbar, slowly and professionally like an old safecracker. He puts his huge
ear against the crate and listens. The crate suddenly shakes from inside. He raises one eyebrow.
Close-up, slow push-in, suspenseful music with a comedic twist, cinematic 3D.
Dialogue in Russian, with lip-sync:
CHEBU (low, husky voice, quiet): "Тикает?.. Нет. Дышит."
```

## Сцена 3. Любовь с первого взгляда (8 сек)
**Референс:** Чебу + попугай

```
The lid flies off, straw bursts into the air. A small parrot with bright emerald-green
feathers, a red chest, yellow cheeks and a fluffy yellow crest pops out, shakes his feathers,
then sees the furry creature with huge round ears. Time slows down: soft pink glow, sparkles,
tiny floating hearts, the parrot's eyes become huge and dreamy, his crest stands up. Romantic
slow motion, close-up on the parrot, cinematic 3D, comedic.
Dialogue in Russian, with lip-sync:
PARROT (squeaky, theatrical male parrot voice, breathless): "Ох... Какие уши..."
```

## Сцена 4. «Я твой» (10 сек)
**Референс:** Чебу + попугай

```
The green parrot flies onto the shoulder of the stern furry creature in the flat cap and
lovingly rubs his head against his cheek, fluttering and chattering non-stop. The furry
creature stands absolutely still with a stone-cold face; only his cigar slowly droops down.
Static medium shot, comedic timing, warm kitchen light, cinematic 3D.
Dialogue in Russian, with lip-sync:
PARROT (squeaky, theatrical, fast, adoring): "Красавчик! Брутал! Мой герой! Я Жорик. Я твой. Навсегда!"
```

## Сцена 5. «Обратно» (8 сек)
**Референс:** Чебу + попугай

```
The stern furry creature calmly takes the green parrot off his shoulder, puts him back into the
wooden crate and closes the lid. He turns away. The lid immediately pops open and the parrot
leans out with a cheeky grin. The furry creature slowly turns back. Medium shot, slapstick
rhythm, warm light, cinematic 3D.
Dialogue in Russian, with lip-sync:
CHEBU (low, husky, firm): "Обратно."
PARROT (squeaky, cheerful): "Не-а!"
```

## Сцена 6. Два болтуна (10 сек)
**Референс:** Чебу + Валькирия + попугай

```
A slender female furry creature with light golden-brown fur, huge round ears, green-hazel eyes,
in a mint-green lace dress with satin bows, enters the kitchen and starts talking very fast.
At the same time the green parrot on the table chatters back at her just as fast. The stern
furry creature in the flat cap sits between them; his head turns left, right, left, and then
he slowly folds his own huge ears down over his eyes. Wide shot, comedic chaos, warm light,
cinematic 3D.
Dialogue in Russian, with lip-sync, overlapping:
VALKYRIA (gentle, warm female voice, very fast): "Чебушка, а кто это? А он кушает? А он кусается? А как его зовут?"
PARROT (squeaky, theatrical, fast): "Жорик! Ем всё! Кусаюсь редко! Люблю его!"
```

## Сцена 7. Дирк (8 сек)
**Референс:** Дирк + попугай

```
A small child furry creature with huge round ears, big brown eyes, bright orange t-shirt and
lab safety goggles on his forehead runs into the kitchen, amazed, pointing at the green parrot.
The parrot puffs up his chest proudly, then dramatically puts a wing on his heart.
Tracking shot at child height, warm light, cinematic 3D.
Dialogue in Russian, with lip-sync:
DIRK (lively, high-pitched boy's voice, excited): "Пап, он говорящий! Можно я ему переводчик изобрету?"
PARROT (squeaky, dramatic): "Не надо. Я и так скажу: люблю!"
```

Slow push-in, quiet heartwarming mood, cinematic 3D.
Dialogue in Russian, with lip-sync:
