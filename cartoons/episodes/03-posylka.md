# Серия 3. «Посылка»

**Сюжет:** Чебу Романович получает посылку. Внутри говорящий зелёный попугай, который с первого
взгляда в него влюбляется. Чебу допрашивает его, но в итоге оставляет.

**Длительность:** ~75 секунд, 9 сцен.
**Референсы:** Чебу Романович, Валькирия, Дирк, попугай (`characters/parrot.md`).

**Короткие обозначения в промтах:**
- `the furry hero` — Чебу (референс-картинка Чебу)
- `the green parrot` — попугай, полностью зелёный (референс-картинка попугая)

---

## Сцена 1. Ящик на крыльце (8 сек)
```
Morning porch with roses. The furry hero in a flat cap, cigar in mouth, opens the door.
A wooden crate with air holes on the doorstep, red stamp "ОСТОРОЖНО. ЖИВОЕ".
He looks around suspiciously.
Medium shot. Warm light. Cinematic 3D.
CHEBU (low, husky, calm): "Я ничего не заказывал."
```

## Сцена 2. Вскрытие (8 сек)
```
Kitchen table. The furry hero opens the crate with a crowbar, like a safecracker.
He presses his huge ear to it. The crate shakes. He raises an eyebrow.
Close-up, slow push-in. Warm light. Cinematic 3D.
CHEBU (low, husky, quiet): "Тикает? Нет. Дышит."
```

## Сцена 3. Любовь с первого взгляда (8 сек)
```
Same crate. The lid bursts open, straw flies. A small, entirely bright green parrot shoots out.
He sees the furry hero: slow motion, pink glow, floating hearts, dreamy eyes.
He flies to him and hugs his neck with both wings. The hero freezes.
Medium close-up. Warm light. Cinematic 3D, comedic.
PARROT (squeaky, theatrical, breathless): "Ох... Какие уши..."
```

## Сцена 4. «Я твой» (8 сек)
```
The green parrot sits on the furry hero's shoulder, rubbing his cheek lovingly.
The hero stands still, stone face. His cigar slowly droops.
Static medium shot. Warm light. Cinematic 3D, comedic.
PARROT (squeaky, fast, adoring): "Красавчик! Мой герой! Я твой. Навсегда!"
```

## Сцена 5а. Допрос (8 сек)
```
The furry hero puts the green parrot on the table and leans in like an interrogator.
One lamp above the table. The parrot shivers, hugging himself with his wings.
Shot-reverse-shot close-ups. Cinematic 3D, comedic.
CHEBU (low, husky, suspicious): "Ты кто такой, зелёный? Кто тебя послал?"
PARROT (squeaky, complaining): "Из Франции сбежал. Холодно там. Газ-то вы перекрыли."
```

## Сцена 5б. «Можно?» (8 сек)
```
The furry hero leans back and waves his hand, smirking.
The green parrot clasps his wings and asks sweetly.
Snap zoom on the hero: eyes go round, the cigar falls from his mouth. Silence.
Cinematic 3D, comedic timing.
CHEBU (low, husky, ironic): "Летел бы к Зелепуперу. У него газа много."
PARROT (squeaky, sweet): "Мы скоро все к вам переедем. Можно?"
```
**Звук:** тишина, стук сигары, сверчок.

## Сцена 6. Два болтуна (8 сек)
```
Valkyria enters the kitchen and talks fast. The green parrot talks back just as fast.
The furry hero sits between them, head turning left-right, then folds his ears over his eyes.
Wide shot. Warm light. Cinematic 3D, comedic chaos.
VALKYRIA (warm, very fast): "Чебушка, это кто? Он кусается?"
PARROT (squeaky, fast): "Жорик! Редко! Люблю его!"
```

## Сцена 7. Дирк (8 сек)
```
Dirk, in an orange t-shirt and goggles on his forehead, runs in and points at the green parrot.
The parrot puffs his chest and puts a wing on his heart.
Tracking shot at child height. Warm light. Cinematic 3D.
DIRK (high-pitched, excited): "Пап, он говорящий!"
PARROT (squeaky, dramatic): "И влюблённый!"
```

## Сцена 8. Финал (6 сек)
```
Night, armchair, one warm lamp. The green parrot sleeps on top of the furry hero's flat cap.
The hero covers him with a handkerchief. A tiny smile.
Slow push-in. Cinematic 3D, heartwarming.
CHEBU (low, husky, very quiet): "Ладно. Живи."
```
**Титр:** «ЧЕБУ РОМАНОВИЧ»
