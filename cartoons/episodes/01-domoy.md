# Серия 1. «Домой»

**Сюжет:** Чебу Романович выходит из тюрьмы. Братья ждут его с «делом», но он отказывается:
его ждёт семья. Дома Валькирия не даёт ему вставить слово, а Дирк встречает папу взрывом.

**Длительность:** ~80 секунд, 10 сцен.
**Референсы:** в каждую сцену загружать картинки тех героев, кто в кадре.
Если герой «поплыл» внешне, добавить в начало промта полный блок из его карточки (`characters/`).

---

## Сцена 1. Ворота (8 сек)
**Референс:** Чебу Романович

```
Night, heavy rain. A massive rusty iron prison gate in a high brick wall slowly creaks open.
A small anthropomorphic furry creature with realistic brown plush fur, huge round ears, tired
dark eyes, a scar over the left eyebrow, wearing a long charcoal overcoat and pinstripe suit,
no hat, steps out alone. He stops, raises his face to the rain and takes a deep breath.
Low-angle shot, slow push-in. Cinematic photorealistic 3D, dark noir, cold blue light,
a single warm gas lamp, wet cobblestones, drifting mist.
```
**Звук:** дождь, скрип ворот, далёкий лай собаки.

## Сцена 2. Братья (8 сек)
**Референс:** картинка банды

```
Headlights of an old black 1920s car cut through the rain on a wet cobblestone street.
Four anthropomorphic furry creatures with huge round ears, dressed as 1920s gangsters in flat
caps, a fedora, long overcoats and pinstripe suits with gold chains, step out of the car and
stand in a row, facing the camera. The smallest one chews a cigar and grins.
Wide shot, slow dolly forward. Dark noir, red neon sign reflections in puddles, rain.
```
**Младший брат:** «С возвращением, Романыч».

## Сцена 3. Кепка (8 сек)
**Референс:** Чебу Романович + банда

```
Close-up. A gangster furry creature in a fedora hands a dark grey wool flat cap to the
world-weary furry creature with huge round ears. He takes it, looks at it for a moment,
then slowly puts it on, adjusting it with two fingers. Faded ring tattoos visible on his fingers.
Rain drops, shallow depth of field, warm lamp light on his face, noir mood.
```
**Длинный брат (за кадром):** «Есть дело. Большое».

## Сцена 4. «Я завязал» (10 сек)
**Референс:** Чебу Романович + банда

```
Over-the-shoulder shot from behind the four gangster furry creatures. The furry creature in
the flat cap and long overcoat shakes his head calmly, pats one brother on the shoulder,
speaks, then turns and walks away alone down the rainy neon-lit street. The brothers look at
each other in silence. Dark noir, rain, neon reflections, steam from a manhole.
```
**Чебу Романович (низко, спокойно):** «Я завязал, пацаны. Меня семья ждёт».

## Сцена 5. Рассвет (8 сек)
**Референс:** Чебу Романович

```
Side tracking shot. The furry creature in the flat cap and long overcoat walks along a country
road. As he walks, night turns into dawn: the rain stops, clouds part, cold blue light turns
into warm golden sunrise, puddles shine. Smooth continuous time transition.
Cinematic 3D, from noir to warm pastel colors.
```
**Звук:** нуарный джаз плавно сменяется тихим аккордеоном, запевают птицы.

## Сцена 6. Валькирия (10 сек)
**Референс:** Валькирия + Чебу Романович

```
A cozy lush garden with a wooden pergola and climbing roses at golden hour. A slender female
furry creature with light golden-brown fur, huge round ears, green-hazel eyes with long lashes,
in a mint-green lace dress with satin bows, is watering flowers. She sees him at the garden
gate, drops the watering can, runs to him and starts talking very fast without stopping,
gesturing. He stands still, expressionless; one of his huge ears twitches.
Medium shot, warm sunlight, soft volumetric light, comedic timing.
```
**Валькирия (тараторит):** «Чебушка! Ты похудел! Ты ел? Я пирог испекла, нет, два пирога,
а соседка Зина говорит, что ты…»

## Сцена 7. Объятие (8 сек)
**Референс:** Валькирия + Чебу Романович

```
Close-up. Mid-sentence, the stern furry creature in the flat cap silently pulls the talking
female furry creature into a hug. She stops talking at once and closes her eyes. Camera slowly
moves to his face: a tiny, barely visible smile appears. Warm golden backlight,
floating pollen, emotional heartwarming moment.
```
**Звук:** тишина, только птицы и ветер в листве.

## Сцена 8. Дирк (8 сек)
**Референс:** Дирк

```
A small child furry creature with fluffy brown fur, huge round ears, big brown eyes, in a bright
orange t-shirt, with clear lab safety goggles on his forehead, runs out of the house across the
garden, holding up a glass flask with bubbling glowing green liquid, excited.
Tracking shot at child height, warm morning light, garden with wildflowers.
```
**Дирк:** «Пап! Смотри, что я изобрёл!»

## Сцена 9. Взрыв (8 сек)
**Референс:** вся семья

```
The three furry creatures stand together in the garden. The glowing flask in the child's hands
suddenly pops with a cartoonish puff of thick green and pink smoke. When the smoke clears, all
three are covered in soot, their fur fluffed up and sticking out, eyes blinking in shock.
Static medium shot, comedic pause, warm sunlight, slapstick humor, no injuries.
```
**Звук:** «пух!», затем тишина и одинокий чирик птицы.

## Сцена 10. Финал (6 сек)
**Референс:** вся семья

```
Close-up of the stern furry creature, face black with soot, flat cap crooked, fur sticking out.
He deadpans a short line. Then the whole family bursts out laughing and hugs. Camera cranes up
and pulls back over the sunlit garden at sunrise. Warm, heartwarming ending.
```
**Чебу Романович (невозмутимо):** «Весь в меня».
**Титр:** «ЧЕБУ РОМАНОВИЧ»
